# Kleynenberg Publishers (`KP` / `naaap.io`)

**Network for Autonomous Audiovisual & Algorithmic Publishing**

Independent Dutch publishing imprint and algorithmic media studio edited and published by Steven Kleynenberg in Delft, The Netherlands. Producing digital books, curated historical photography monographs, visual satire, broadsheet editions, and algorithmic music and cinema.

**Live site:** https://kp.kleynenberg.com

## Focus Areas

* **Curated Historical Photography Monographs & Broadsheets:** Fine-art monochrome editions and archival print studies (including *GIGA! Garbage In, Garbage Out: Une Étude en Ordure et Lumière*).
* **Visual Satire & Parody:** Cultural and comic parodies published via Google Play Books (including *Niet van Hergé - De Grappigste, Scherpste en Vreemdste Kuifje-Parodieën*).
* **Typography & Digital Book Production:** Classical Garamond typesetting, EPUB production, and legacy PostScript/TrueType font restoration.
* **Algorithmic Music, Cinema & Audio:** Instrumental fusion productions, 4K experimental film timelapses, and spoken-word audio (*The Kleynenberg Perspective*).
